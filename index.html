FM-1 Android Mic Lock

Lock the phone mic first, then connect the FM-1 and route to Android's active USB output.

1

Disconnect the FM-1

Start with nothing plugged into USB-C. This gives Chrome the best chance of opening the built-in phone microphone first.

2

Lock the phone microphone

Chrome opens one microphone stream and this page keeps that exact stream alive after USB is connected.

Mic not locked.
3

Connect the FM-1 over USB-C

After the mic is locked, plug in the FM-1. Do not press Scan Inputs again.

—Locked input
—Visible audio devices
Waiting for USB connection.
4

Start the FM-1 bridge

The site outputs to Android's current/default media route. If Android has switched playback to the FM-1, the FM-1 should receive it.

Bridge stopped.
—AudioContext rate
—Base latency
This is a best-effort Android routing trick. Chrome may still force the capture route to USB when the FM-1 is attached. The page deliberately never reacquires the microphone after Step 2.
Use this from HTTPS (for example GitHub Pages) in Google Chrome on Android.
